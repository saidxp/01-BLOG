# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and developers who publish technical work, document learning, follow peers, and discuss ideas. They need a calm, readable environment for creating and consuming developer-focused content across desktop and mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

01Blog is a blogging community for learning journeys and technical publishing. Success means posts, authorship, discussion, and community activity remain easy to create, scan, read, and manage without visual noise or loss of existing application behavior.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, premium, and calm. The experience should also feel modern, readable, developer-friendly, and community-oriented.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic Bootstrap appearance, SaaS-dashboard styling, social-media cloning, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, neon treatments, giant radii, decorative overload, and pixel-for-pixel imitation of DEV.to.

## Design Principles

- Put content first and keep navigation supportive.
- Make article titles the strongest scannable element while keeping metadata quiet.
- Use a restrained, reusable visual system with one primary accent and semantic state colors.
- Preserve familiar interaction patterns and every existing Angular behavior contract.
- Remove secondary context before primary content as screens narrow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, explicit control labels, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, fluid layouts, and intentionally designed mobile states. Existing disabled, loading, error, and conditional states must remain perceivable and operable.
